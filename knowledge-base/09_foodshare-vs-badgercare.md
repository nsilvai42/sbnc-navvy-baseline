# FoodShare vs. BadgerCare Plus: what is the difference, and can I get both?

You can apply for both — and you should if you want help with food and health coverage. They are two separate programs with separate eligibility, so the State of Wisconsin reviews each one on its own.

Here is the quick difference:

- **FoodShare** is Wisconsin's name for SNAP. It helps you buy food. Benefits load onto a card called the Wisconsin QUEST card (an EBT card you use at approved stores like a debit card).
- **BadgerCare Plus** is Wisconsin's Medicaid program. It is health coverage — it helps pay for doctor visits, prescriptions, and other medical care.

Because they are different programs, each one looks at your situation on its own:

- Both depend on your income and household size. BadgerCare Plus also looks at which group you fall into (for example, adult, parent, child under 19, or someone who is pregnant) and whether you live in Wisconsin.
- You might be eligible for one and not the other, or for both. Being approved for FoodShare does not mean you will be approved for BadgerCare Plus, and vice versa.

**Your next step:** If you are interested in either kind of help, apply for both. You can do it in one place — Wisconsin's ACCESS system handles applications for FoodShare and BadgerCare Plus. Your screener estimate here can give you a sense of what you may be eligible for before you apply.

A reminder on the wording: a screener or this chatbot can only tell you what you *may* qualify for. The State of Wisconsin makes the final decision after you submit your application.
