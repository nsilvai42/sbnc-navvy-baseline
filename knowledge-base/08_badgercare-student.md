# Can I get BadgerCare Plus (health coverage) as a student?

Yes, you may be able to get BadgerCare Plus, even if you are a student. BadgerCare Plus is Wisconsin's Medicaid program, which gives free or low-cost health coverage to people who qualify.

Here is the most important part if you are 19 or older: BadgerCare Plus looks at your tax household, and being claimed as a dependent on your parents' taxes doesn't automatically disqualify you — your own income is only counted in certain situations. This is different from FoodShare, which has special student rules. And, as of now, there is no "20 hours a week" type work rule for BadgerCare Plus.

Whether you qualify depends on your income, your household size, and your category (for example, an adult, a parent, a pregnant person, or a child). Children and pregnant people can qualify at higher income levels than other adults. Run your screener estimate to see where you may land, then apply through the State of Wisconsin's ACCESS system. The State makes the final decision after you apply.

A few situations that often help students:
- Under 19? You may be able to stay on a parent's BadgerCare Plus case, even if you live away at school.
- Going to school out of state? You can still qualify if you are a Wisconsin resident.
- Were you in foster care (or subsidized guardianship/kinship care) on your 18th birthday? If you're a Wisconsin resident, you may be eligible up to age 26, even with income that would otherwise be too high. The State confirms this when you apply.

You do not need to type your income, Social Security number, or other private details into this chat. You will share those securely when you apply.
