Three things to do now

1Save your confirmation number or take a screenshot.
2Watch for emails, mail, and calls from unknown numbers — that's often the state.
3Respond to any requests by the deadline. Missing it can delay your application.

Tip: Save your agency’s phone number as a contact before or after they first reach out, so you don’t miss any of their messages. Calls may still come from a different number, so check voicemail too.

Navvy can't see your application status. For updates, use BenefitsCal or contact your county directly.
