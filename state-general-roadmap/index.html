Decide in the product meeting

Decision 1

What sets the recommended order?

The screens use the SNAP estimate as the example. Confirm the final rule for programs without comparable dollar estimates. Keep every eligible program visible.

Decision 2

Where should progress be collected?

Roadmap, email/SMS, or both. The data model should be shared: capture, persist, link to the student record, and timestamp.

Decision 3

Can the roadmap render immediately?

If the screener moves in-house, show it directly after results. Otherwise, agree how students return without creating another high-friction destination.

Decision 4

What contact guidance can V1 support?

Use the static phone tip now. Add direct device integration or maintained agency numbers only after technical feasibility and ownership are confirmed.

Decision 5

How will denial patterns be reported?

Self-report among respondents only, with nonresponse visible. Agree privacy rules and minimum cell sizes before geography or demographic cuts.

Decision 6

What is the first testable slice?

A practical V1 is recommend-first screens plus one progress check-in. Denial branching and tailored follow-up depend on the shared back end.
