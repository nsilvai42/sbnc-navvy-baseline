Change 3 · Heard back

Choosing "I heard back" opens a branch. Denied or "asked for more" shows a calm, fixable-denial support box; Denied also offers an optional, self-reported reason.

How did it go?

A denial often isn't final.

Many denials are just a missing document or a form that needs another signature — and those are usually fixable.

1Read the notice for the reason. It will say what was missing or why you were denied.
2Send the missing documents through your state's portal, or call your county or agency to ask what they need.
3If you think the decision is wrong, reapply or ask for a review. Sometimes called an appeal or fair hearing — the notice explains how.
4Still stuck? Get help. Your county office can walk you through it, or reach us at help@studentbasicneeds.com.
