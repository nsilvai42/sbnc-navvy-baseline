Surface 1 · Illinois worked example

State-general roadmapNew: self-serve step navigation

Four journey states, and every step is reachable at any time: done bars carry "Go back," the current step carries a self-report advance button, and upcoming steps carry an "I'm on this step" jump link — students can skip ahead whenever they want. Only the step 1→2 transition can be data-driven (screener completion); after that the app has no signal, so every step change is self-reported. Front-end page swaps only, no data capture.
