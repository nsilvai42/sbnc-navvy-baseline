Usage rules

  • Timeline connectors (desktop): done→done solid green; the gradient green→coral appears ONLY on the segment entering the current step; current→upcoming coral→gray (line-strong).
  • One current step per roadmap; the current card carries the page's single primary CTA.
  • Locked steps never show preview/body copy — eyebrow + title only.
  • Never mix the two radius systems: ticket corners are desktop-state-roadmap only.
  • Step navigation (added 2026-08-02): every done bar carries a green "Go back" link (desktop: right end of the bar; mobile: right of the DONE label — never beside the title, which can't shrink at 375px). The current card may carry one self-report advance button ("I've submitted my application — show step 3"); the final step never does. Locked desktop cards show the padlock hint "Opens after step N"; mobile locked rows stay bare.
  • All step-navigation controls are front-end state flips only — no data capture, no storage (2026-07-28 agreement). In the mocks they link between the four state files; in the app, bind them to the roadmap's user-interaction-only step state. Keep the reassurance footnote under the list ("Updating your step here only changes this page — never your actual application.").
