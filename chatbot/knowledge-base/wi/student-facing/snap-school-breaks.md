# What happens to FoodShare over school breaks

Good news: your FoodShare often continues during summer, winter, and spring break — your benefits don't automatically stop just because classes are out. (One important exception is below if you qualify through work-study.)

Here's the rule. Your FoodShare generally continues over a school break as long as two things are true:

- You stay enrolled for the next term. As long as you plan to come back when school starts again, you still count as a student during the break, even if you're not taking classes.
- You still meet all the other FoodShare rules (like income, household size, and any student work or exemption rules that apply to you).
- **If you qualify only through work-study:** that exemption is paused during any break of a full month or longer, unless you actually do work-study during the break. Over shorter breaks, it keeps going.

FoodShare is Wisconsin's name for SNAP, the program that helps you buy groceries with a Wisconsin QUEST card (an EBT card that works like a debit card for food).

A few things to keep in mind:

- If you graduate, drop out, or don't plan to return next term, you may no longer count as a student, and your situation could change.
- If anything about your life changes over the break — your income, your hours at a job, your address, or who lives with you — report it. Wisconsin asks you to keep your case info up to date, and changes can affect your benefits.
- Keep an eye out for any renewal or check-in notices from the state during the break. These usually come by mail or through your ACCESS account (access.wi.gov), so respond on time so your benefits don't lapse.

The State of Wisconsin makes the final call on whether your benefits continue, so the best place to confirm your specific case is Wisconsin's official FoodShare site, where you can also manage your case through ACCESS (access.wi.gov).
