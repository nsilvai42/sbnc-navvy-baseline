# How can I apply, and who decides if I qualify?

Here's the short version: you fill out an application, and the **State of Wisconsin makes the final decision** about whether you get benefits. Nobody else can approve you, and any estimate you see before then is just an estimate.

Here's how the steps usually go:

- **Screener (you're often here first).** This gives you an *estimate* of whether you may qualify for FoodShare (Wisconsin's food benefit) or BadgerCare Plus (Wisconsin's health coverage). It is only a guess based on what you enter. It is **not** an application, and it does not approve or deny you.
- **Your roadmap / next steps.** This shows you what to do next and what to expect. It is not the official application.
- **Apply through ACCESS.** ACCESS is Wisconsin's official online system for benefits applications. Your real application goes here. You can start it and come back to finish later.
- **Submit.** Once you send it in, the state reviews it.
- **State follow-up.** The state may ask for documents, schedule a short interview, or mail you a notice. Answering quickly keeps things moving.

Where does Navvy fit in? I help you understand each step, figure out your next action, and make sure you give the state everything it needs so your application doesn't stall. I do not decide if you qualify.

Two things to keep in mind: you **may qualify** for these programs depending on your income and household size, so check your screener estimate for your situation. And only the State of Wisconsin can make it official.

Source: WI DHS FoodShare Handbook (P-16001) §2.1.2 (application process / 30-day timeline); WI DHS — Apply for Benefits (dhs.wisconsin.gov/forwardhealth/apply.htm); applications submitted through Wisconsin ACCESS (access.wi.gov).
