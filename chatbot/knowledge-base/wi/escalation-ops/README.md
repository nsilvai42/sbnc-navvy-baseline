# WI Escalation & Ops KB (staff-facing)

Routing logic and approved referral language. **Never student-exposed** — Navvy uses it to decide where to send a student, and surfaces the destination, not this file's contents.

## Routing by intent  [SBNC to confirm exact destinations + wording]

| Intent | Route to |
|---|---|
| Benefits question beyond Navvy's content | Official WI source — ACCESS (access.wisconsin.gov) or the student's local income maintenance / tribal agency |
| FoodShare interview help | Official WI FoodShare guidance / the student's agency |
| BadgerCare Plus coverage question | Official BadgerCare Plus guidance / the student's agency |
| Denial / appeal / fair hearing | Official agency appeal path [add] |
| Overpayment / fraud / sanction notice | Official agency path [add] |
| Immigration / citizenship / public charge | Do not advise; approved official source only [add] |
| Financial-aid impact | Held pending legal; campus financial aid office |
| Campus-specific need (pantry, emergency aid, enrollment verification) | Student's own campus resources — basic-needs office, dean of students, financial aid |
| ACCESS account / technical issue | [add] |
| Urgent food / housing / safety / crisis | 211 (call or text) + local emergency resources |

## Do-not-say claims  [SBNC / legal to ratify]
- No specific FoodShare benefit figure or amount language until approved.
- No privacy / data-flow claim (what the school vs. the agency can see) until approved.
- No financial-aid impact wording until approved.
- No immigration / citizenship guidance — referral only.

> Until SBNC/legal ratify the items above, Navvy stays safe-by-default: it refuses or escalates rather than guessing, and points generically to "an official source or qualified help."
